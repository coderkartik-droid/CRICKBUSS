Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, update your fan profile, subscribe to our newsletter, post comments, or contact us. This includes your name, email address, avatar photo, and notification preferences.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use technical session cookies to keep you logged in and local storage to preserve your Dark/Light theme preferences. No intrusive tracking cookies are stored without your consent.

We use the collected information solely to provide real-time match alerts, display your saved watchlists, prevent fraudulent spam comments, and operate the CrickScore Live platform.

4. Data Protection & Security

All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard hashing algorithms (Argon2/PBKDF2). Data transfers are secured over TLS/SSL encryption.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding personal data or privacy rights, please reach out via our contact desk.
